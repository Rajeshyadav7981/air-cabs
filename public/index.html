      Help & FAQs

      Got questions?

      Are toll and parking charges included?

      Tolls and airport parking are optional extras and charged at actuals. Everything else is included in the shown fare.

      What happens if my flight is delayed?

      We track your flight. Your driver waits free for up to 45 minutes after landing.

      How do I pay?

      Pay the driver by cash or UPI at the end of the trip, or pay online in advance.

      Can I cancel my booking?

      Yes — cancellation is free up to 3 hours before pickup.
